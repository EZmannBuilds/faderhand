# Knowledge pack

Lessons a private training lab has learned about producing music with Faderhand, cleared for shipping. The lab's
tools (a music generator, analysis engines, controlled experiments in Live and offline, a lesson library) are not
part of Faderhand and are not needed to use this. Only the lessons ship.

- `LESSONS.md`: the lessons for agents and people, grouped by stage (sketch, arranged, mix).
- `lessons.json`: the same lessons as data (schema `faderhand.knowledge/1`).

## What a lesson is

Each one is either:
- a **measured finding**: from a controlled experiment where one thing changed at a time and the cause of every change
  was known (offline renders and/or renders in Ableton Live); or
- a **sourced convention**: from published producer interviews and guides, with the sources named.

Each carries its genres, its stage, a confidence (a judgement, not a probability), what was found, a technique, how to
do and check it with Faderhand's tools, and its limits.

**None is a rule, and none outranks the listener.** Unless a lesson says otherwise, nobody has listened to the
result: a measured effect is not the same as a heard improvement. When you apply a lesson, say which one, measure the
result as its Faderhand line says, and report what you did not hear.

## What is never in it

- the music generator, its outputs or anything about running it;
- anyone's personal preferences, any project's decisions, song material or audio;
- hypotheses and model suggestions that have not been tested;
- anything from a model or dataset whose licence forbids commercial use.

The lab regenerates both files when lessons are added or revised. Edit lessons there, not here.
